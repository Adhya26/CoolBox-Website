ABOUT COOLBOX

Comfort should fit into your everyday life.

CoolBox is a personal cooling system concept created to address the discomfort of extreme heat in small living spaces. We aim to make personal cooling more accessible, practical and convenient.

✓ Designed with college hostel students and compact rooms in mind.
✓ Focuses on personal comfort rather than cooling an entire room.
✓ Created with affordability, portability and energy-conscious use as priorities.
THE PROBLEM

Hot rooms. Limited choices.

During extreme summer heat, students living in hostels and small rooms can struggle to stay comfortable. Traditional cooling options may not always be practical or affordable.

🌡️

Uncomfortable heat

High temperatures in small rooms can make studying, relaxing and sleeping uncomfortable.

💸

High cooling costs

Air conditioning and other cooling options may be expensive for students on limited budgets.

🏠

Limited facilities

Many hostels and shared rooms do not provide individual access to air-conditioned spaces.

HOW IT IS INTENDED TO WORK

A simple cooling concept.

CoolBox is based on an airflow and evaporative-cooling concept. The final performance depends on the finished design and testing.

01

Air intake

The fan draws surrounding air into the device through its air-intake area.

02

Cooling process

The concept uses an evaporative-cooling approach to help cool the passing air.

03

Airflow delivery

The fan directs the airflow towards the user's personal space for added comfort.
